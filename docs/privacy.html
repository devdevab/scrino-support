Privacy policy · Updated October 14, 2026

Data collection

Scrino has no app accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. Screenshots and annotations stay on your Mac. Scrino does not send screenshots, annotations, clipboard contents, or search terms to a photo service.

Screenshots and the clipboard

Scrino captures the selected window or screen and processes the image locally on your Mac. The styled screenshot is copied to the system clipboard. Screenshots are saved when you choose Save or enable automatic saving to a folder you select. Scrino does not upload screenshots to a server.

Other applications can access content you paste or share with them. macOS clipboard sharing, backups, and other system services follow your system settings and their own privacy policies.

Optional photo backgrounds

The bundled backgrounds and images you import from your Mac work locally. More on Unsplash opens the official Unsplash website in your regular browser. If you download a photo there, use Choose Your Own to import it into Scrino. Your browser and Unsplash govern any website browsing, cookies, or downloads under their own privacy settings and policies. Scrino does not use an embedded website, native Unsplash search, or a Cloudflare photo service in this release.

The included photographs and any image you import are processed locally. Unsplash’s privacy policy applies when you visit its website.

Permissions and local settings

Screen Recording permission is used to capture windows from other apps. Scrino does not record audio and does not require Accessibility access. You can revoke Screen Recording access in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Scrino stores your keyboard shortcut preset, cursor preference, and chosen default background locally on your Mac. If you choose an imported image as your default background, Scrino stores a local copy in its app storage so it remains available after restart. Permission denial is remembered during the current app session to avoid repeated prompts. This information is not sent to the developer.

Support and this website

These pages and the support issue tracker are hosted by GitHub. GitHub’s privacy statement applies when you visit or use those services. Support issues are public; please do not include private screenshots, passwords, or other sensitive information.

Changes and questions

If Scrino’s data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date. For questions about this policy, contact the developer through GitHub.
