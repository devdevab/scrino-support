A little help from Scrino.

Beautiful window screenshots. Free for everyone.

Take your first screenshot

  1. Open Scrino on your Mac. Requires macOS 14 or later.
  2. Press ⌘ 4, hover over the window you want, and click. Press Escape to cancel.
  3. Use ⌘ 5 to capture the screen containing your pointer. Each screenshot gets padding, rounded corners, and a soft shadow, then goes straight to your clipboard.
  4. Press ⌘ 6 and drag to capture a selected area. Hold Space while dragging to move the selection. Press Escape to cancel.
  5. Press ⌘ 3 and drag to capture an iPhone-shaped portrait area.
  6. Paste it into another app with ⌘ V.

Adjust the look

Click the pencil in the small screenshot preview, or open the studio from Scrino’s menu bar eyes. Choose a background and adjust padding, shadow, and corners. Open More for photo, solid-color, gradient, transparent, and imported backgrounds. Use the star or Use as Default to save your choice for future captures and restarts. More on Unsplash opens your regular browser; download a photo there and import it using Choose Your Own. Click Copy after making changes, or Save to export a PNG.

Annotate and customize

Use Text, Pen, Arrow, Rectangle, Highlight, or Blur in the studio. Drag a rectangle with Blur to hide sensitive details, and adjust its strength. Choose a color and size, and use Undo or Redo as needed. Use the selection tool to move annotations. Copy or Save exports your edits.

Open Capture settings from the gear or the menu bar eyes to choose keyboard shortcut presets and include or hide the cursor. The defaults are Command–4 for window selection and Command–5 for the whole screen, and Command–6 for a selected area.

Screen Recording permission

Allow Scrino in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording (called Screen Recording on some macOS versions). Restart Scrino if macOS asks. This permission lets Scrino capture other windows. Scrino does not record audio.

The shortcut isn’t working

Keep Scrino running, check Screen Recording access, and check the selected preset in Capture settings. If another app uses the same global shortcut, choose a different preset in Capture settings. The shortcut is Command–4, without Shift.

Where are my screenshots?

Each capture is copied to the clipboard. Captures are saved when you choose Save or enable automatic saving to a folder you select in Settings. Filenames begin with Scrino and include the local date and time. A new screenshot replaces the previous clipboard contents. Protected windows may not be capturable.

Still need help?

Report a problem or ask a question on GitHub. Include your macOS version, Scrino version, and the steps that led to the issue. GitHub issues are public, so leave out private information.
